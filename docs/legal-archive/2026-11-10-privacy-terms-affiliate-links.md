# Privacy, terms and affiliate links (/legal/)

Archived copy. In effect from 2026-11-10 until 2026-11-16, when a "How we use AI" section was added. Source: git commit d1af722^.

## The short version

There's nothing to sign up for here: no accounts, no newsletter, no forms. We don't collect your name or email address unless you email us yourself, and we never sell or share anything about you.

## What happens when you visit

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser, the page you asked for) to deliver pages and block abuse, like any web host. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser and never sent to us.

## Affiliate links and cookies

Many posts link to places where you can book or buy things: Klook, GetYourGuide, Expedia, Hotels.com, Viator, Amazon, eSIM and SIM providers like Holafly and Airalo, travel insurers, and a few others. These are affiliate links. If you book or buy through one, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

So partners can credit us for a booking, our affiliate networks (Impact and Partnerize) run small tracking scripts on this site, and a partner may set a cookie when you click through or use a booking widget in a post. Once you're on their site, their privacy policy and terms apply, not ours.

Commissions never decide what we write about or recommend.

## Using what you read here

Prices, opening hours and rules in Japan change. We share everything in good faith as a starting point for your own planning, so check the current price before you book.
