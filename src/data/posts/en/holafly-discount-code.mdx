## Two different products, two different discounts

Holafly sells two things, and the code does something different on each one:

- **Destination and regional eSIMs.** A one-off eSIM for a single country or region, unlimited data for a set number of days, bought once per trip. The code takes **5% off**.
- **Holafly Plans.** A subscription eSIM covering 160+ destinations on one install, billed monthly, quarterly, or annually. The code takes **10% off**.

| Plan | Standard price | With {telecomAffiliates.holafly.code} |
|---|---|---|
| Plans Light (25GB, 160+ destinations) | $49.90/month | ~$44.91/month |
| Plans Unlimited (unlimited data, 160+ destinations) | $64.90/month | ~$58.41/month |
| Plans Ultra (unlimited data, 160+ destinations, plus doctor chat, flight/luggage claim handling, and wellness add-ons) | $90.95/month | ~$81.86/month |
| Always On (1GB/month, 150+ destinations) | Free add-on after any Plan | No code needed |

The 10% rate applies across the whole Plans lineup, Light, Unlimited, and Ultra alike, not just the two older tiers.

Annual billing on a Plan runs about 20% below the monthly list price, and the code stacks on top of that rather than replacing it, Unlimited billed annually plus the code lands around $46.73 a month, the biggest saving on this page if you're traveling enough to justify a year up front.

## Buy it if, skip it if

Holafly's strongest case is a multi-country trip or a family keeping several devices online without buying a new SIM at every border, which describes most of our travel. It's not the cheapest eSIM on the market dollar for dollar, a local physical SIM can run two to four times cheaper for a long stay in one country, so if Japan is the entire trip and you're there a month or more, it's worth pricing a local option against it rather than defaulting to Holafly out of habit. For a one to three week Japan trip, which is most of what this site is written for, the convenience of installing it before you fly and having it working the moment you land is worth the premium.

## A few honest notes

- The 5% and 10% rates are Holafly's affiliate terms for this code, not a number we rounded.
- The code is reusable, not a one-time coupon, and works for returning customers too.
- It applies to Plans subscriptions including the China Plan (which bundles a VPN), not just destination eSIMs.
- None of Holafly's products support phone calls. Data is the product; the Unlimited Plan's number receives SMS only.
- Pricing above is in USD; currency shown at checkout can vary by region.
