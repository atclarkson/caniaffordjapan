We've already [written up how Zauo works using our Osaka visit](/blog/zauo-fishing-restaurant-osaka-catch-your-own-fish-cost/), the real pricing, the no catch and release rule, all of it. This one's older and smaller: a real December 4, 2022 evening at a Zauo location in Tokyo, near the end of a six week stretch in Japan that was about to end with a flight to Bangkok three days later. We don't have the exact neighborhood confirmed in our own records, the chain has several Tokyo branches, so this is the story of that one night rather than a location guide.

## How the night broke down

- **A TGI Fridays stop first.** The evening opens there before dinner proper, a familiar chain in a city full of them, the kind of easy stop a tired family makes between a day out and the night's real plan.
- **The walk to Zauo.** Tokyo at night on foot, the restaurant itself coming into view as the destination for the night's main event.
- **Into the restaurant.** Indoor tanks built into the floor, the same setup we'd later see again in Osaka: rent a rod, drop a line, whatever comes up is dinner.
- **Lily catches a fish.** The actual catch, the moment the whole stop was built around, five years old and reeling something real out of a tank in the middle of a restaurant.
- **Walking in Tokyo at night.** After dinner, no rush back, just more of the city on foot.
- **A crane game to close it out.** The kind of small, unplanned stop that fills the last few minutes of a video before the credits roll.

## Why this one's worth telling even without fresh pricing

We didn't keep a receipt from this specific night, the real numbers and the full mechanics of how Zauo works are already broken down properly in [the Osaka post](/blog/zauo-fishing-restaurant-osaka-catch-your-own-fish-cost/), worth reading there instead of us guessing what a December 2022 Tokyo bill might have come to. What this one adds instead is the earlier half of the story: Zauo wasn't a one-off Osaka discovery, it was already a real stop on our very first extended Japan trip, more than six months before the Osaka visit most of this site's Zauo content is built around. If you're weighing whether to try it on a Tokyo leg of a trip instead of waiting for Osaka, the honest answer from two visits in two different cities is the same either way: yes, and bring a kid who's up for getting her hands wet.
