If you're weighing whether to add this to an Osaka itinerary, here's the number first: adult admission starts at 1,800 yen (around $12), with current Klook listings running closer to $11.49. Kids (elementary and junior high) start at 500 yen, preschoolers get in free, and it's closed on the second and fourth Monday of every month. That's the real baseline, confirm the exact current rate on Klook before booking since it moves with the season.

## It's a real garden, not a building

teamLab's two Tokyo museums, [Planets](/blog/teamlab-planets-tokyo-ticket-price/) and [Borderless](/blog/teamlab-borderless-tokyo-with-kids/), are both purpose-built indoor spaces. This one is different: Nagai Botanical Garden is a genuine 240,000 square meter public garden in Higashisumiyoshi Ward, open during the day like any other park, and teamLab's installation only exists after sunset. The permanent night exhibition opened July 29, 2022, following a renovation of the park, and it turns the living garden itself, around 1,200 real plant types, into the artwork. The light projections respond to wind, rain, and whoever's walking through, a different mechanic entirely from either Tokyo museum's fixed indoor rooms.

## Hours shift with sunset, and that's not a minor detail

Because the art is tied to actual daylight, the opening time moves through the season rather than staying fixed: evening starts have run anywhere from around 6:30pm in September to closer to 7:45pm in early summer, according to the venue's own season-by-season schedule. Last entry has consistently been listed at 8:30pm with a 9:30pm close, though at least one source puts last entry as late as 9pm on weekends during cherry blossom season, worth double-checking for your specific dates rather than assuming one number holds all year. No food is sold inside the exhibition itself, plan to eat at one of the restaurants near Nagai Station before or after.

## The garden changes the art, which is the actual hook

Because the installation lives inside a working botanical garden, what's on display shifts with what's blooming. Cherry blossom season has brought a dedicated "Resonating Trees" installation built around the garden's weeping cherry trees, and an August run has featured roughly 20,000 illuminated white sunflowers lit up after dark. Planets and Borderless are both fixed exhibits you can describe once and have it stay accurate; this one is worth checking the current season's specific installation before a visit, since what's there in April isn't what's there in August.

## Getting there

Nagai Station serves it from three different lines: the Midosuji subway line, the JR Hanwa line's own Nagai Station, and Kintetsu Minami-Osaka line's Harinakano Station, with reported walking times from Nagai Station itself running from roughly 10 minutes depending on which exit.
