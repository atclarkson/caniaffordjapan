## How to use it

Click through to Klook using a link on this site (any of them work, they all carry our affiliate tracking), then type **ADAMANDLINDSKLOOK** into the promo code field at checkout. The discount is applied by the code at checkout, not by the link itself, so you do need to enter it, clicking through alone doesn't apply anything.

A few honest notes on these:

- The 10% and 3% tiers need a $50 total cart, a single teamLab ticket or a single small activity usually won't hit that on its own. Bundling a couple of bookings together (say, a teamLab ticket plus a transit pass) gets you there.
- The 1% tier has no stated minimum and covers a specific list of activities, teamLab Planets TOKYO and teamLab Borderless among them, so it's the one that reliably applies to the posts we've written about those.
