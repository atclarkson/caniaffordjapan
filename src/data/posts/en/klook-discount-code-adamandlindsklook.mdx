## How to use it

Click through to Klook using a link on this site (any of them work, they all carry our affiliate tracking), then type **ADAMANDLINDSKLOOK** into the promo code field at checkout. The discount is applied by the code at checkout, not by the link itself, so you do need to enter it, clicking through alone doesn't apply anything.

- The 10% and 3% tiers need a $50 total cart, a single teamLab ticket or a single small activity usually won't hit that. Bundling a couple of bookings together (say, a teamLab ticket plus a transit pass) gets you there.

## A few booking tips

- teamLab and Disney both sell out on weekends and holidays. Book at least a week ahead rather than assuming a same-day slot will be there.
- A JR Pass bought through Klook is an exchange order, not a ticket, the lead traveler needs ID at the exchange counter to redeem it.
- Some venues take a mobile voucher straight off your phone, others want it printed, check the specific activity's listing before you assume either way.
