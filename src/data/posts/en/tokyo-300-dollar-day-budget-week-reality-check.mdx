$401.34. That's the real, on-screen Day 1 total from a week we tried to keep under a loose $300/day goal for a family of five, tracked as the costs happened rather than added up afterward. Over target before a single full day of sightseeing had even started.

## Why Day 1 blows the number on its own

That $401.34 covers close to what we've [written up separately as a full itemized Day Zero breakdown](/blog/landing-in-tokyo-day-zero-cost-breakdown/): luggage forwarding from the airport, a pocket wifi or eSIM setup, the Keisei Skyliner into the city, a first dinner at a yakitori izakaya, and a late-night 7-Eleven run before bed. None of that is sightseeing, it's pure logistics, and it's also a fixed cost that happens exactly once per trip rather than once per day. Spread that same $400-ish across a full week instead of counting it against Day 1 alone, and the daily average already looks a lot less alarming. A single bad-looking day one is a framing problem as much as a spending problem. The [eSIM versus pocket wifi math](/blog/esim-vs-sim-vs-pocket-wifi-japan/) is also one of the easier pieces of that number to shrink before you land.

## The days that pull the average back down

The week's plan leaned hard on Tokyo's real split between free and paid, the same split we keep finding neighborhood by neighborhood. A day built around [Senso-ji Temple](/blog/senso-ji-temple-free-asakusa-tokyo/) and the [Kappabashi kitchen-supply street](/blog/kappabashi-kitchen-town-plastic-food-samples-worth-it/) costs close to nothing beyond food. A day around [Ueno's zoo](/blog/ueno-zoo-shinobazu-pond-boats-tokyo-cost/) and [Ameyoko market](/blog/ameyoko-name-origin-ueno-food-stalls-cost/) is mostly a food-and-wandering budget, not an admission-ticket one. Odaiba, home to a paid [teamLab Borderless ticket](/blog/teamlab-borderless-tokyo-with-kids/) and a bay cruise, has lost its one truly free headline draw since this trip was filmed: the life-sized Unicorn Gundam statue that used to anchor [DiverCity's plaza](/blog/doraemon-time-square-hello-kitty-divercity-odaiba/) came down for good on August 31, 2026, so that specific free photo-op no longer applies if you're planning a visit today.

## The honest answer

We don't have a confirmed dollar figure for each of the other six days, the video tracks the running total on screen as it happens rather than stating a clean daily number for every single day, and we're not going to invent six figures we don't have. What we can say plainly: some days land well under $300, a free-temple-and-market day especially, and some days, arrival day most of all, blow straight past it. A $300/day target works best read as a weekly average to aim for, not a hard ceiling you're meant to hit every single morning. Treat one expensive logistics day as the cost of starting the trip, not as proof the whole week failed.

## Worth knowing if you're planning your own

If you're chasing a daily number like this yourself, budget the arrival day separately from the rest of the week rather than folding it into the same average, it skews everything. Judge the trip on where the week landed as a whole, not on whichever single day happened to be on screen when you did the math.
