Grandma and Grandpa flew out to join us for this one, seven of us total, which made the Hakone Free Pass an easy call before we even looked at the math: one ticket, everybody covered, no juggling individual fares for a group that size. Turns out the math backs it up anyway.

## What the pass actually costs

Odakyu sells the pass directly from Shinjuku Station:

| | 2-day pass | 3-day pass |
|---|---|---|
| Adult | 7,100 yen | 7,500 yen |
| Child (6 to 11) | 1,600 yen | 1,850 yen |

That covers the round trip train from Shinjuku to the Hakone area on the regular express (not the reserved-seat Romancecar, that's a separate 1,200 yen adult / 600 yen child add-on if you want it), plus unlimited rides for the whole pass period on eight different modes of transport inside Hakone: local trains, buses, the cable car, the ropeway, and the Lake Ashi pirate ship cruise. It also gets you a discount at around 70 museums and restaurants in the area.

## What paying separately would have cost

Skip the pass and buy each leg as you go, and here's roughly what stacks up for one adult doing a full day (Shinjuku round trip, the ropeway, and one pirate ship crossing):

| Leg | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto, round trip, regular express | 2,540 yen |
| Ropeway, round trip | 3,000 yen |
| Lake Ashi pirate ship, one way | 1,000 to 1,200 yen |
| A couple of local bus rides around the area | 800 yen |
| **Total** | **around 7,300 to 7,500 yen** |

That already lands at or above the 7,100 yen pass price, for exactly one round trip on each mode of transport. We didn't do that, we rode the bus more than once, took the ropeway both ways to see Fuji properly, and did the boat crossing. Every ride past the first one is where the pass actually starts saving money instead of just breaking even.

## What a day with the pass actually looks like

Local buses in Hakone get packed, we ended up standing room only more than once with three generations of us hanging onto the same rail. Nobody minded, the ropeway view of Fuji from above the valley was worth the whole trip on its own, and Lake Ashi's floating torii gate at Hakone Shrine lit up red after dark was a genuine surprise, we hadn't planned around seeing it.
