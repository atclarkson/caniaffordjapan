Three ways to get online in Japan: an eSIM on your own phone, a physical SIM card, or a pocket wifi hotspot you carry and share. For one person traveling light, an eSIM wins on convenience almost every time. For our family of five, the math works out differently, and it's worth doing that math before you buy anything.

## Pocket wifi

## The actual math, for us

We don't buy individual data plans for the girls, they use tablets on wifi and the pocket wifi covers them. So the real comparison for a 7 day trip is 2 adult eSIMs versus 1 shared pocket wifi:

| Option | Cost for 7 days |
|---|---|
| 2 Airalo eSIMs (one per adult) | $53.00 |
| 1 Japan Wireless pocket wifi (covers the whole family) | about $34.30 before the 10% code |

For a solo traveler or a couple who wants each phone independently connected, an eSIM per person is simpler and the price gap barely matters. For a family sharing devices anyway, one pocket wifi is both cheaper and one less thing to set up per phone. That's the version we actually use.
