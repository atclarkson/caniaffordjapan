Straight to the number: official adult admission is 2,400 yen on a regular day (2,700 yen on a peak date), while Klook's own listing runs closer to $15-16, call it roughly half the door price once the exchange rate settles. Kids aged 4-15 are 1,200 yen regular, under-3s free. Confirm the current rate on Klook before booking, these figures move.

## The one that uses your whole body

teamLab's other three Japan museums are either a water-based walk-through (Tokyo's [Planets](/blog/teamlab-planets-tokyo-ticket-price/)), a maze of floating light orbs (Tokyo's [Borderless](/blog/teamlab-borderless-tokyo-with-kids/)), or a living garden that becomes art after dark ([Osaka's Botanical Garden](/blog/teamlab-botanical-garden-osaka-ticket-price/)). Fukuoka's version is different again: it's explicitly built as an athletic space, uneven and soft terrain, high-speed spinning balls, balance stepping stones, designed around grasping the installation physically rather than just walking through light. A separate "Catching and Collecting Forest" area leans more into quiet discovery, a real-time digital butterfly piece among its opening works. Heels or anything unstable on your feet aren't going to work here, worth knowing before you dress for the day.

## Where it sits

teamLab Forest is the 5th floor of BOSS E.ZO FUKUOKA, an entertainment complex that opened in July 2020 right next to PayPay Dome, the same stadium we've [been to for a real Hawks game](/blog/paypay-dome-fukuoka-vs-tokyo-dome-baseball-cost/). The complex itself costs nothing to walk into, only each individual attraction inside charges admission. No dedicated parking at E.ZO itself, the dome's own lot is the one to use. On foot, it's roughly 15 minutes from Tojinmachi Station on the Fukuoka City Subway, or a direct bus from Hakata Station.

## Hours and how long to plan for

Sources agree on the regular pattern: weekdays 11:00-19:00, weekends and holidays 10:00-19:00, though at least one listing shows weekday hours running later to 20:00, and hours shift on PayPay Dome event or night-game days since the two buildings share foot traffic. Check the official page for your actual date rather than trusting one number. Most visitors clear the whole space in 60 to 90 minutes, admission is timed to the slot printed on your ticket, so arriving on time matters more here than at a walk-in attraction.
