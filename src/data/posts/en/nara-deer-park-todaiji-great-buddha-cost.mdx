Nara was one of our first stops the first time we came to Japan, back in November 2022, and it's stuck with us since. Deer wander loose through the park and the streets around it, completely free to see, feed, and get photobombed by. The Great Buddha inside Todai-ji, a short walk into the same park, is not free, and the two costs are easy to mix up if you haven't looked them up before you go.

## Nara Park and the deer: free

Nara Park itself has no gate, no ticket, and no hours you need to work around. The deer, over a thousand of them, roam the park and the surrounding streets on their own, and looking at them, walking near them, even having one nose around your bag, costs nothing.

Feeding them is the one thing that costs money: **shika senbei**, the deer crackers sold from stalls around the park, run 200 yen for a stack of about 10. That price has held since 2019, when it went up 50 yen for the first time in 28 years.

## Todai-ji's Great Buddha Hall: not free

The Daibutsu-den, the hall that actually holds the Great Buddha statue, charges admission:

| | Adult (13+) | Child (6 to 12) |
|---|---|---|
| Great Buddha Hall only | 800 yen | 400 yen |
| Combined with the Todai-ji Museum | 1,200 yen | 600 yen |

That's the one fee in an otherwise free afternoon, and it's worth paying. The statue is enormous in a way photos genuinely undersell, and you're inside the temple complex either way just walking through the park to reach it.

## Getting to Nara from Osaka or Kyoto

Nara has no airport and no bullet train station of its own, you're getting there on a regular commuter line either way, and it's cheap either way.

| From | Line | Time | One-way fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| Osaka (Namba) | Kintetsu Nara Line, Rapid Express | about 40 min | 680 yen |
| Kyoto Station | JR Nara Line, Miyakoji Rapid | about 45 min | 720 yen |

Both are direct, no transfers, running every 15 to 30 minutes depending on the time of day. Kintetsu also runs a Limited Express from Osaka that cuts the ride to about 30 minutes for a reserved-seat surcharge, roughly 1,200 to 1,300 yen total, not worth it for a trip this short unless you specifically want the guaranteed seat.

## What the day actually cost us

For our family that day, two adult tickets to the Great Buddha Hall (Harper was 2, well under the child ticket's 6 to 12 range) plus a few packs of deer crackers split between the girls was the entire spend for the afternoon. Nara works as a day trip from Osaka or Kyoto rather than somewhere you need to stay overnight, so the only other real cost is the train, 680 yen from Osaka or 720 yen from Kyoto, each way, per person.
