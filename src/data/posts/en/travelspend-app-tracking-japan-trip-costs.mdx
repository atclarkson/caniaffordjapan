The obvious assumption about a travel budget app is that it works like Mint or YNAB, linked straight to your bank and cards, pulling in every charge automatically. TravelSpend doesn't do that, no bank connection at all, every expense gets entered by hand. That's not a missing feature, it's the whole design: no API trying and failing to parse a foreign-currency charge from a Japanese convenience store, works offline on a train with no signal, and syncing anything to a bank means handing over account access we'd rather not give a travel app in the first place.

## What it does

Log an expense in yen, it converts to home currency at the current rate automatically. Charts break spending down by category, accommodation, food, transit, activities, whatever categories you set up. Entries sync between phones on the same trip, so Adam and Lindsay both log without creating duplicates. Free covers basic tracking; Premium adds cost splitting, detailed analytics, multiple simultaneous trips, and cloud sync.

## Splitting costs, a real Tokyo example

During a two week Tokyo stay with relatives, the split-cost feature is what made shared spending workable: whoever paid for a meal or a train pass logged it against the group, and the app tracked who owed whom across the whole stay instead of someone keeping a running tally on their phone's notes app. That's the kind of multi-family cost splitting a Japan trip with grandparents or friends tends to need, and it's specifically a Premium feature, not the free tier.

## How we actually log things

Not every receipt in real time. Lindsay usually catches up on the previous day's spending each morning, a couple of minutes scrolling back through what happened. Bigger purchases, a hotel booking, a big dinner, get logged the moment they happen instead of waiting. We review the whole week's spending together on Sundays, which is where the category charts earn their keep, seeing food or transit creeping up over a few days is a different signal than noticing one expensive afternoon in isolation.

Completely, and specifically because it doesn't try to be more than a travel budget tracker.
