We've done both. Tokyo Disneyland and DisneySea in September 2025, and Universal Studios Japan enough times that one of the photos below is captioned "for the fourth time." Neither park has one ticket price. Both use date based pricing that moves with the calendar, so the number you see depends entirely on which day you pick.

## What a 1-day adult ticket actually runs

| Park | Adult | Junior/Child |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo Disneyland or DisneySea (one park, not both) | 7,900 to 10,900 yen | Child (4 to 11) 4,700 to 5,600 yen, junior (12 to 17) 6,600 to 9,000 yen |
| Universal Studios Japan | 8,900 to 11,900 yen | From 5,700 yen |

A few things worth knowing before you pick a date:

- Tokyo Disney Resort sells Disneyland and DisneySea as separate single park tickets, doing both parks in one trip means two separate admissions, not a combo ticket.
- Neither park sells tickets at the gate anymore. You book a specific date in advance, and that locks in that day's price.
- Kids 3 and under get into Tokyo Disney Resort free.

## Which one, if you can only pick one

Tokyo Disney Resort is the better fit for younger kids and anyone who wants the classic Disney parade and castle experience, and doing Disneyland and DisneySea back to back gives you two very different parks for the price of two tickets. Universal Studios Japan is the one our girls ask to go back to, Super Nintendo World and the Harry Potter area pull kids who are a little older and want bigger rides, not just character meet and greets. We've gone back to USJ more times than any other single attraction in Japan, that alone tells you where our money actually goes.
